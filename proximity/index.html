What is Proximity?

Proximity is a principle of design that refers to the spatial relationship between the elements of the design. The rules of proximity are pretty simple: things that are related should be nearer to each other, and things that are unrelated should be placed further from each other.

Good vs. Bad Design Examples

Organization:

Proximity helps organize a design by creating clear separation between different elements, making the layout easier to navigate and understand.

Connection:

Proximity connects related elements by placing them close together, helping users quickly see which items belong together.
