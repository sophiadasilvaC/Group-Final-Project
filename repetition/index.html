What is Repetition?

Repetition in design refers to the recurring use of visual elements to create consistency and familiarity. Repetition isn't just about copying elements—it's about maintaining harmony and guiding the viewer through a design.

Good vs. Bad Design Examples

Pattern:

Pattern in design is the repeated use of shapes, lines, or colors in a predictable way. It adds structure and visual interest to the design.

Rhythm:

Rhythm is a combination of elements repeated, but with variations.
